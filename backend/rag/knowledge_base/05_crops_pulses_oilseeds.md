# Soil and climate requirements of pulses and oilseeds

## General notes on pulses
Pulses fix atmospheric nitrogen with Rhizobium bacteria in root nodules, so they need only a small starter dose of nitrogen. Seed treatment with Rhizobium and phosphate-solubilising bacteria is commonly recommended. Most pulses do not tolerate waterlogging and need well-drained soils. Including pulses in rotation improves soil fertility for the next crop.

## Chickpea (Bengal gram)
Soil: well-drained loams to clay loams; black soils with stored moisture are ideal for rainfed rabi chickpea. pH about 6.0 to 8.0. Sensitive to waterlogging and to salinity. Season: rabi (sown about October to November), cool dry weather. A major crop of north Karnataka.

## Pigeonpea (red gram, tur)
Soil: deep, well-drained loams, red and black soils; pH about 6.0 to 7.5, sensitive to waterlogging and salinity. Climate: tolerant of drought once established; about 600 to 1000 mm rainfall. Season: kharif, long duration (about 150 to 200 days); often intercropped with sorghum, maize, groundnut or millets.

## Green gram (mung) and black gram (urad)
Soil: well-drained loams and sandy loams; black gram also suits heavier soils. pH about 6.2 to 7.5. Short duration (about 60 to 90 days); kharif, rabi in the south, and summer (zaid) under irrigation. Sensitive to waterlogging.

## Groundnut (peanut)
Soil: well-drained, light-textured sandy loams and red soils are best because pods develop underground; heavy clays make harvesting difficult and reduce pod filling. pH about 6.0 to 7.5. Calcium supply during pegging is important; gypsum is commonly applied at flowering. Season: kharif rainfed and rabi or summer irrigated.

## Sunflower
Soil: wide range, best on deep, well-drained loams and black soils; pH about 6.5 to 8.0, moderately tolerant of salinity. Photo-insensitive, so it can be grown in kharif, rabi and summer. Boron deficiency can reduce seed set.

## Soybean
Soil: well-drained loams and black soils with good organic matter; pH about 6.0 to 7.5; does not tolerate waterlogging. Season: kharif (sown with the monsoon onset). Major crop of central India and parts of north Karnataka.

## Mustard and rapeseed
Soil: loams to clay loams; pH about 6.0 to 7.5, tolerant of moderate salinity. Season: rabi, cool dry weather. Requires sulphur for oil content.
