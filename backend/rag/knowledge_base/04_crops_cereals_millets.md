# Soil and climate requirements of cereals and millets

## Rice (paddy)
Soil: grows on many soils but clay, clay loam and silty clay soils that hold water are best; tolerates waterlogging. pH range about 5.0 to 8.0, best about 5.5 to 6.5. Climate: warm and humid, high water requirement (roughly 1000 to 2000 mm per season depending on method). Season: mainly kharif (June to November); rabi or summer rice where irrigation is available. Water-saving methods include alternate wetting and drying and the System of Rice Intensification (SRI). Common nutrient issue: zinc deficiency, especially in alkaline or continuously flooded soils.

## Wheat
Soil: well-drained loams and clay loams; also black soils under irrigation. pH about 6.0 to 7.5; tolerates up to about 8.0. Climate: cool growing season (about 15 to 25 C) and warm, dry ripening. Season: rabi (sown about late October to December, harvested March to April). Critical irrigation stage: crown root initiation (about 20 to 25 days after sowing), then tillering, flowering and grain filling.

## Maize
Soil: deep, well-drained loams to sandy loams rich in organic matter; does not tolerate waterlogging. pH about 5.5 to 7.5. Climate: warm (about 21 to 30 C), moderate water need. Season: kharif mainly; rabi and summer under irrigation. Critical stages for water: tasselling and silking. Responds strongly to nitrogen; zinc deficiency is common on calcareous and low organic matter soils.

## Finger millet (ragi)
Soil: grows on red sandy loams and lateritic soils, tolerant of poor soils; pH about 5.0 to 8.2. Climate: rainfed in about 500 to 1000 mm rainfall; drought tolerant. Season: kharif mainly (in Karnataka sown about June to August). Staple crop of south Karnataka; nutritious, rich in calcium.

## Sorghum (jowar)
Soil: deep clay loams and black soils with good water-holding capacity for rabi (post-rainy) sorghum; lighter soils for kharif sorghum. pH about 6.0 to 8.5. Climate: hot and dry tolerant, about 400 to 1000 mm rainfall. Season: kharif and rabi; rabi sorghum on stored soil moisture is common in north Karnataka black soils.

## Pearl millet (bajra)
Soil: light sandy and sandy loam soils, tolerant of low fertility; pH about 6.5 to 8.0 and tolerant of some salinity. Climate: very drought tolerant, about 250 to 700 mm rainfall, high temperature. Season: kharif. Does not tolerate waterlogging.
