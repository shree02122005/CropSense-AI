# Soil and climate requirements of commercial, vegetable and plantation crops

## Cotton
Soil: deep black clay soils with high water-holding capacity are classic cotton soils; also grown on alluvial and red soils with irrigation. pH about 6.0 to 8.0; moderately tolerant of salinity. Needs good drainage. Season: kharif (sown with monsoon), long duration (about 150 to 180 days), needs a frost-free period and dry weather at boll opening.

## Sugarcane
Soil: deep, well-drained loams to clay loams; black and alluvial soils. pH about 6.5 to 7.5. Long duration (about 10 to 18 months), very high water requirement (roughly 1500 to 2500 mm); drip irrigation saves water. Heavy feeder of nitrogen and potassium.

## Tomato
Soil: well-drained loams and sandy loams rich in organic matter; avoid waterlogged and very heavy soils. pH about 6.0 to 7.0. Climate: warm, about 20 to 27 C is ideal; fruit set is poor above about 32 C or below about 15 C. Can be grown year-round in much of south India with irrigation; kharif, rabi and summer crops are common. Calcium supply and regular irrigation reduce blossom-end rot. Staking and mulching improve yield and quality.

## Onion
Soil: well-drained loams, sandy loams and light black soils; pH about 6.0 to 7.5; sensitive to waterlogging and salinity. Season: kharif, late kharif and rabi; rabi onion stores best. Shallow roots need frequent light irrigation.

## Potato
Soil: loose, well-drained sandy loams to loams; slightly acidic pH about 5.0 to 6.5 is preferred (higher pH can increase scab disease). Cool weather (about 15 to 20 C during tuber formation). In Karnataka grown in kharif in Hassan and nearby districts and in rabi elsewhere.

## Chilli
Soil: well-drained loams and black soils; pH about 6.0 to 7.5; sensitive to waterlogging. Warm humid climate; kharif and rabi.

## Banana
Soil: deep, well-drained, fertile loams rich in organic matter; pH about 6.0 to 7.5; does not tolerate waterlogging or salinity. Warm humid climate, high water and potassium requirement. Grown year-round with irrigation.

## Coconut
Soil: wide range including laterite, red, alluvial and coastal sandy soils, provided drainage is good and soil is deep; pH about 5.2 to 8.0. Needs warm humid climate and regular water supply.

## Arecanut
Soil: deep, well-drained laterite, red and alluvial soils; pH about 5.5 to 7.0; sensitive to waterlogging and drought. Grown in coastal and Malnad Karnataka with irrigation or high rainfall.

## Coffee
Soil: deep, well-drained, fertile forest soils rich in organic matter; slightly acidic pH about 5.5 to 6.5. Grown under shade at elevations of roughly 500 to 1500 m in the Western Ghats (Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru, Hassan). Arabica prefers higher elevations and cooler climate than Robusta.

## Mango
Soil: deep, well-drained soils from sandy loam to clay loam, including red and lateritic soils; pH about 5.5 to 7.5; avoid waterlogged and saline soils. Needs a dry period before flowering.
